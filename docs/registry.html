Ducati Monster (for Ella)

This is a practical mode of transportation fit for a fuel crisis. OK that’s only partially true. Lets start again. This is a reliable and safe mode of transportation… OK that’s definitely not true. What is true is decades of motorsport heritage and the most well known name in two wheeled transportation. I’m part Italian and it’s just about the only Italian word I know. Reliable? Practical? You’re asking the wrong questions! Is a Porsche reliable? A Patek Philippe practical? I don’t know - wrong questions.

But wait a minute, is the value proposition for a Ducati really as ostentatious as a Porsche’s or Patek Philippe’s? They all seem to carry a similar calibre of excess but the Ducati Monster cost less then the cheapest new car you can buy and gives you the performance of a supercar. The Ducati Monster gets better gas economy than a Toyota prius. The Ducati Monster oil changes cost $50. So yes, a Ducati is a inessential luxury item. But it still might be darn good value.

P.S. the answers to the questions you should be asking are

  • desmodromic valves,
  • 0.3 hp/lb (0.5 kW/kg),
  • 45,000 km service intervals,
  • supercar performance from a vehicle cheaper than a Suzuki Ignis (look it up).
